App support

For playback problems, include the title, episode if applicable, app version and device model. Do not send passwords, authentication tokens or payment details.

Privacy and deletion

Favorites and playback progress are local: use Clear activity in your profile to remove them. A request about server-side records is separate; explain what you want reviewed or deleted. There is no account to delete in the current version.

Copyright concerns

For a rights claim, identify the work, the relevant title in FePlay, your relationship to the rights holder and a way to contact you.
