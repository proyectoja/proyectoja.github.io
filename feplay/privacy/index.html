Privacy policy

Updated: November 25, 2026. This policy describes the native FePlay app, operated by PROYECTO JA, Android package proyecto.ja, and its companion website.

1. Information on your device

Favorites, your saved list, playback position, watched status and language preferences are stored on your device. They are not synchronized with a personal account. FePlay does not currently require account registration, publish user comments or download videos for offline viewing. Your profile lets you clear local activity. Clearing app storage or uninstalling also removes local data, subject to your device's behavior.

2. Anonymous activity and statistics

Our service assigns a random, signed anonymous installation/browser identifier to prevent duplicate votes and count activity. The backend stores pseudonymous hashes for ratings, reactions and daily playback deduplication. Votes can be changed or removed from the relevant title. Clearing local activity does not automatically remove server-side ratings or aggregate counters. We do not ask for your name, contacts, photos, microphone or precise location.

The app records entries when it opens or returns to the foreground, and a new visitor-day when the UTC date changes. These records are separate from the live online counter. Daily-visit deduplication keys expire within 48 hours and entry retry receipts after seven days; expired keys are removed during subsequent activity. Long-term statistics retain aggregate totals, not a public visitor list. Aggregate counters may be viewed in the app without a password. Anonymous vote and playback-deduplication records are kept for counter integrity and service operation; privacy requests are handled through the contact below.

3. Online counter and network information

FePlay uses Chatbro, an external provider, to estimate the number of connected participants. The app connects directly to that service over HTTPS using a separate random identifier saved on your device. It does not send Chatbro your FePlay authentication token, advertising ID, name, favorites, playback history or media-source addresses. No chat window or downloaded chat script is needed for this counter. The provider receives the network information needed to deliver the service, including your IP address. Its count and expiry behavior depend on the provider; a connection failure is not displayed as a fabricated zero.

Chatbro privacy policy

FePlay's activity tables do not store raw IP addresses or GPS coordinates. If a trusted infrastructure provider supplies an approximate country, we may retain country-level aggregate statistics; otherwise the country remains unknown. Small new country groups are not displayed. Hosting, security, media delivery and advertising providers may process network information under their own policies; we do not promise that every provider has no logs.

4. Advertising and consent

The native app may display Google AdMob advertising. Depending on configuration, region, age-related treatment and consent, Google's advertising SDK may collect or share IP-derived approximate location, advertising/device/app identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic information for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Advertising therefore does not process only an advertising ID. Production ad requests require the consent SDK's permission to request ads. Where required, privacy choices can be reviewed from your profile. Advertising IDs can also be reset or deleted in Android settings.

5. Playback, images and external links

Playing video or radio and loading images connects your device to the provider hosting that resource, which receives the network information necessary to deliver it. External links, Google Play and sharing applications have their own privacy practices. This companion website does not load the native advertising SDK or the app's live counter; its hosting provider may maintain operational access logs.

6. Permissions and children

The app needs Internet access and playback-related capabilities, including a media foreground service for live audio and picture-in-picture when used. These do not authorize recording your microphone or accessing personal files. The release configuration blocks camera, microphone, contacts, media-library/storage and precise/coarse Android location permissions. FePlay is intended for a general audience, not specifically for children. A family-friendly catalog or content rating does not by itself define the target age group. Advertising treatment and consent must match the audience declared in Google Play. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us.

7. Security, your choices and contact

Production API connections must use HTTPS. No Internet service can guarantee absolute security. Contact PROYECTO JA for access, correction, deletion or other applicable privacy rights; explain your request without sending passwords, payment details or authentication tokens. We may need proportionate information to locate a pseudonymous record. Aggregate statistics that do not identify you may remain. We do not sell your favorites or personal viewing progress.
