Payments and refunds

Refund Policy

This policy explains how to ask for a refund for an Arcan Player subscription.

Last updated November 20, 2026

1. Payment provider

Subscriptions are purchased through PayPal or Paddle. The provider shown on your receipt processes the payment and may handle the refund transaction under its own procedures.

2. Paddle's minimum 14-day request period

For every payment processed by Paddle, including a renewal charge, you may request a refund for at least 14 calendar days from the date of that charge. We will honor this minimum request period; longer periods or additional rights under consumer law or Paddle's rules continue to apply.

3. How to submit a request

For Paddle, use the support/refund link in your receipt or contact Paddle Support. For PayPal, open the transaction in your PayPal account and use its Resolution Center or contact PayPal Support. If you need help identifying the purchase, email Arcan Player Support with the receipt/order reference, payment provider, purchase date, plan and the email or username used to activate it.

The project administrator’s support email is being configured.

4. Processing and privacy

The payment provider may need to verify the transaction and will return approved funds to the original payment method. Processing and bank posting times vary. Never send your full card number, password or security code by email. This policy does not limit mandatory consumer rights or any more generous refund terms shown by the provider.

5. Cancellation is separate

A refund request does not automatically cancel a recurring subscription, and cancelling a subscription does not automatically refund an earlier charge. Cancel future renewals with the provider before the next billing date. Contact support if you need help.
