Frequently asked questions

What can I play with Arcan Player?

Arcan Player is a desktop media player for compatible audio and video. The current app also describes YouTube, downloads and a floating mini player as Premium features. Use only media and sources you are authorized to access.

How do I activate my subscription?

After checkout, enter the username and email for your app account. Copy the subscription code shown on the page and enter it in Arcan Player to activate Premium.

Where can I buy a subscription?

Visit the Arcan Player subscription page to compare the current offer and complete checkout with PayPal or Paddle. After payment, link the purchase to your username and email to receive the activation code.

How can I cancel or request a refund?

Cancel recurring billing with the provider you used before the next renewal date. For Paddle purchases, you may request a refund for at least 14 calendar days after the charge; use the link in your receipt or Paddle support. See the Refund Policy for details.

Why will a file or media source not play?

Playback depends on the media format, source availability and your computer. Check that the source is available and supported, then try again. Arcan Player cannot make unavailable or unsupported media playable.

Does Arcan Player provide videos or downloads?

Arcan Player is a player, not a content service. It does not provide third-party videos, music or downloads. You are responsible for using content you own or are authorized to access.
