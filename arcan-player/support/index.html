Frequently asked questions

How do I add an M3U or M3U8 playlist?

In Arcan Player, open the live-playlist area, choose the add option, enter a name and the playlist URL, then confirm. You can also use the app’s Web option from a phone or computer on the same local network while the Roku app is open, then save an authorized M3U/M3U8 source.

How do I play a video, song or stream?

Choose a playlist, select an item from its contents and press OK to open the preview. Use the Play control or the Roku remote’s Play button for full-screen playback. Audio and video controls depend on the selected content.

How do I manage Premium?

Open Premium in Arcan Player to review the offer and start or restore a Roku Pay purchase. Billing and subscription status are managed by Roku. The final price and any offer terms appear in Roku Pay before you confirm.

How can I cancel a Roku subscription?

Cancel through your Roku account’s subscription-management settings or from the Roku device’s subscription settings. Follow Roku’s current instructions and cancel before the next renewal date to prevent a future charge. Roku controls billing and refund requests for Roku Pay purchases.

Why won’t a file or stream play?

Playback depends on Roku format support, the stream’s encoding, your network and whether the source is online and permits access. Check the URL, try again later, and confirm the source works on a compatible Roku device. Arcan Player cannot make an unsupported or unavailable source playable.

Where does Arcan Player get its channels and videos?

Arcan Player is a player, not a content service. It does not include or provide third-party channels, videos, music or playlists. You add sources that you own or are authorized to access.
