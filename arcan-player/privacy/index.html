Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Arcan Player may handle, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

Last updated November 20, 2026

1. Who is responsible

Arcan Player is an independently operated application and website. The project administrator handles privacy requests through the support contact listed below.

2. Information you provide

If you buy a subscription, the checkout provider processes your payment. After checkout, the subscription page asks for a username and email address so the purchase can be linked to your app account. The page stores the username, email, subscription reference and related status in your browser local storage.

3. Payment and subscription verification

PayPal or Paddle processes payment details under its own privacy notice. Arcan Player does not receive or store your full card number. To verify a purchase, the page sends the subscription reference, payment provider, username and email to the project verification service over HTTPS. The hosting, email and verification providers may process technical connection data needed to provide their services.

4. Website and app storage

The subscription page uses browser local storage to remember the language, username, email, subscription reference and activation code. The app may store its own preferences and activation status on your computer. You can clear browser storage through your browser settings; this may remove saved codes and details from that browser.

5. How information is used

Information is used to process and verify a purchase, link a subscription to an app account, provide support, investigate payment errors and meet legal obligations. It is not used to sell your personal information.

6. Retention and deletion

Browser-stored information remains until you delete it or clear browser storage. The verification service and payment providers may retain transaction records as needed to administer subscriptions, resolve disputes and meet legal or accounting duties. Contact support about information held by the project; some records may need to be retained by law.

7. Third-party services and links

The subscription flow uses PayPal, Paddle, website hosting and a subscription-verification service. The app may also connect to media sources you choose. These providers are governed by their own terms and privacy notices.

8. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information. No website, device or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Do not send payment-card details to support by email.

9. Children

The subscription service is not directed to children who cannot lawfully make purchases. A parent or guardian who believes a child has provided personal information should contact the project administrator.

10. Your choices and policy updates

You can decline to provide optional details, clear local browser storage, and contact support with privacy questions or requests. The current version and effective date of this policy will be posted here.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the Arcan Player project administrator using the details on the Support page.
