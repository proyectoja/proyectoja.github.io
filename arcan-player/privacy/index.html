Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Arcan Player may handle, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

Last updated October 15, 2026

1. App and responsible contact

Arcan Player is an independent media-player application for Roku. The Arcan Player project administrator is responsible for this policy and for responding to privacy questions through the support contact listed below.

2. Information the app handles

Depending on the features you use, the app handles playlist names and URLs, media-source addresses, favorites, app preferences, playback selections and subscription status. If you choose to use subscription verification, information needed to identify or validate that purchase—such as a Roku transaction reference, account detail or device-related identifier—may be processed by Roku and the service used by the app provider. The static pages on this website do not ask you to create an account or submit personal information.

3. Local storage, lists and backups

Playlist details, favorites and preferences are stored on your Roku device. Some playback or guide data may also be cached on the device, subject to Roku storage and app behavior. Premium backup and restore tools let you export or restore supported list and favorite data. A backup you export is under your control; protect it as you would other personal files.

4. Connections to media sources

When you add or play an external playlist, stream, video, audio item or guide, the Roku device connects to the source you selected. That source and its hosting provider may receive connection information such as your IP address, request details and other technical information needed to deliver the content. Those providers control their own data practices. Use only sources you are authorized to access.

5. Subscriptions and Roku Pay

Purchases, billing, renewal options and cancellation are handled through Roku Pay under the terms shown by Roku. The app may check subscription status with Roku and the app provider’s verification service so that purchased features can be made available. The official website is informational only: it does not connect to Roku Pay, process subscriptions or collect payment details.

6. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by the app and website. No device, network connection or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. Keep your Roku software updated and use trusted media sources and networks.

7. Retention and deletion

Information saved by the app generally remains on the Roku device until you remove it in the app, reset app data, or remove the app, subject to Roku’s storage behavior. You can delete exported backup files from the place where you saved them. Roku, media sources and subscription-verification providers may retain information under their own policies.

8. Children

Arcan Player is a general-purpose media player and is not designed to request personal information from children. Parents or guardians should supervise a child’s use of external sources and subscription features. If you believe a child has provided personal information in connection with the app, contact the project administrator.

9. Third-party services

Third parties may include Roku and Roku Pay, the hosting provider that serves this static website, media and playlist providers you choose, and services used to verify subscriptions. Their terms and privacy notices apply to their services. Arcan Player does not control how those third parties handle information.

10. Policy updates

This policy may be updated as the app or its practices change. The current version and its effective date will be published on this page. Review it periodically.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the Arcan Player project administrator using the support details on the Support page.

The project administrator’s support email is being configured.
